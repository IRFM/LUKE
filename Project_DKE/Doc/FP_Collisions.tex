In the Fokker-Planck equation, the diffusion and covection elements are
bounce-averaged according to the expressions (\ref{diffbav})-(\ref{convbav}%
), which gives, using (\ref{Dcoll})-(\ref{Fcoll}),  
\begin{eqnarray}
D_{pp}^{\text{C}(0)} &=&\left\{ A(p)\right\}  \\
D_{p\xi }^{\text{C}(0)} &=&0  \nonumber \\
D_{\xi p}^{\text{C}(0)} &=&0  \nonumber \\
D_{\xi \xi }^{\text{C}(0)} &=&\left\{ \frac{\xi ^{2}}{\Psi \xi _{0}^{2}}%
B_{t}(p)\right\}   \nonumber
\end{eqnarray}
and the convection components 
\begin{eqnarray}
F_{p}^{\text{C}(0)} &=&-\left\{ F(p)\right\}  \\
F_{\xi }^{\text{C}(0)} &=&0  \nonumber
\end{eqnarray}
and therefore 
\begin{eqnarray}
D_{pp}^{\text{C}(0)} &=&A(p) \\
D_{p\xi }^{\text{C}(0)} &=&0  \nonumber \\
D_{\xi p}^{\text{C}(0)} &=&0  \nonumber \\
D_{\xi \xi }^{\text{C}(0)} &=&\frac{\lambda _{2,-1,0}}{\lambda }B_{t}(p) 
\nonumber
\end{eqnarray}
and 
\begin{eqnarray}
F_{p}^{\text{C}(0)} &=&-F(p) \\
F_{\xi }^{\text{C}(0)} &=&0  \nonumber
\end{eqnarray}

The bounce coefficient $\lambda _{2,-1,0}$ is defined as (\ref{lkm}) 
\begin{equation}
\lambda _{2,-1,0}=\lambda \left\{ \frac{\xi ^{2}}{\Psi \xi _{0}^{2}}\right\} 
\end{equation}

For reference to the litterature, note that we could also perform the
following transformation

\begin{eqnarray*}
\left\{ \frac{\xi ^{2}}{\Psi \xi _{0}^{2}}\right\} &=&\left\{ \frac{1-\Psi
\left( 1-\xi _{0}^{2}\right) }{\Psi \xi _{0}^{2}}\right\} \\
&=&\left\{ 1-\frac{1}{\xi _{0}^{2}}\left( 1-\frac{1}{\Psi }\right) \right\}
\\
&=&1-\frac{\Delta _{b}}{\xi _{0}^{2}}
\end{eqnarray*}
with 
\[
\Delta _{b}\equiv \left\{ 1-\frac{1}{\Psi }\right\} 
\]
