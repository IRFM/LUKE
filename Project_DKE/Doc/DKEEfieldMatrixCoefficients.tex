L'operateur du champ electrique est caracterise par \begin{eqnarray*}
\widetilde{F}_{p}^{\text{E}} & = & q_{e}\xi E_{\Vert}\\
\widetilde{F}_{\xi}^{\text{E}} & = & -q_{e}\sqrt{1-\xi^{2}}E_{\Vert}\end{eqnarray*}


On obtient ainsi

\begin{eqnarray*}
\widetilde{F}_{p}^{\text{E}(0)} & = & \sigma q_{e}\left\{ \frac{\sigma\xi}{\Psi(\theta)\xi_{0}}\xi E_{\Vert}\right\} \\
 & = & q_{e}\left\{ \frac{\xi^{2}}{\xi_{0}^{2}}\right\} \xi_{0}E_{\Vert,0}\\
 & = & \xi_{0}\frac{\widetilde{s}^{*}}{\lambda\left(\xi_{0}\right)}q_{e}E_{\Vert,0}\end{eqnarray*}
\begin{eqnarray*}
\widetilde{F}_{\xi}^{\text{E}(0)} & = & -q_{e}\left\{ \frac{\xi^{2}}{\Psi^{3/2}\xi_{0}^{2}}\sqrt{1-\xi^{2}}E_{\Vert}\right\} \\
 & = & -q_{e}\left\{ \frac{\xi^{2}}{\xi_{0}^{2}}\right\} \sqrt{1-\xi_{0}^{2}}E_{\Vert}\\
 & = & -\sqrt{1-\xi_{0}^{2}}\frac{\widetilde{s}^{*}}{\lambda\left(\xi_{0}\right)}q_{e}E_{\Vert,0}\end{eqnarray*}
 Sous forme discrete, les coefficients lies au champ electrique constant
qui interviennent dans la matrice $\widetilde{\overline{\overline{M}}}^{\left(0\right)}$
sont \[
\widetilde{\overline{\overline{D}}}^{\text{E}(0)}\rightarrow\left\{ \begin{array}{l}
\widetilde{D}_{pp,i+1,j+1/2}^{\text{E}(0)}=0\\
\widetilde{D}_{pp,i,j+1/2}^{\text{E}(0)}=0\\
\widetilde{D}_{p\xi,i+3/2,j+1/2}^{\text{E}\left(0\right)}=0\\
\widetilde{D}_{\xi p,i+1/2,j+3/2}^{\text{E}\left(0\right)}=0\\
\widetilde{D}_{p\xi,i+1/2,j+1/2}^{\text{E}\left(0\right)}=0\\
\widetilde{D}_{\xi p,i+1/2,j+1/2}^{\text{E}\left(0\right)}=0\\
\widetilde{D}_{p\xi,i-1/2,j+1/2}^{\text{E}\left(0\right)}=0\\
\widetilde{D}_{\xi p,i+1/2,j-1/2}^{\text{E}\left(0\right)}=0\\
\widetilde{D}_{\xi\xi,i+1/2,j+1}^{\text{E}(0)}=0\\
\widetilde{D}_{\xi\xi,i+1/2,j}^{\text{E}(0)}=0\end{array}\right.\]
 et \[
\widetilde{\overline{\overline{F}}}^{\text{E}(0)}\rightarrow\left\{ \begin{array}{l}
\widetilde{F}_{p,i+1,j+1/2}^{\text{E}(0)}=\xi_{0j+1/2}\frac{\widetilde{s}_{j+1/2}^{*}}{\lambda\left(\xi_{0j+1/2}\right)}(q_{e}E_{\Vert,0})\\
\widetilde{F}_{p,i,j+1/2}^{\text{E}(0)}=\xi_{0j+1/2}\frac{\widetilde{s}_{j+1/2}^{*}}{\lambda\left(\xi_{0j+1/2}\right)}(q_{e}E_{\Vert,0})\\
\widetilde{F}_{\xi,i+1/2,j+1}^{\text{E}(0)}=-\sqrt{1-\xi_{0j+1}^{2}}\frac{\widetilde{s}_{j+1}^{*}}{\lambda\left(\xi_{0j+1}\right)}(q_{e}E_{\Vert,0})\\
\widetilde{F}_{\xi,i+1/2,j}^{\text{E}(0)}=-\sqrt{1-\xi_{0j}^{2}}\frac{\widetilde{s}_{j}^{*}}{\lambda\left(\xi_{0j}\right)}(q_{e}E_{\Vert,0})\end{array}\right.\]
