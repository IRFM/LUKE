We consider a kinetic equation generally expressed in conservative form in
the phase space $\left( p,\xi ,\psi ,\theta \right) $. The gyroangle $%
\varphi $ can be ignored since the kinetic equation was gyroaveraged. $f$ is
the guiding-center distribution function. In addition, the toroidal angle $%
\phi $ is ignored unsder the assumption of axisymmetry. The kinetic equation
can be expressed as
\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial f}{\partial t}+\nabla _{\left( p,\xi ,\psi ,\theta \right) }%
\cdot {\bf S}\left( f\right) =0
\end{equation}
where the phase space flux ${\bf S}$ is decomposed into a diffusive and a
convective part
\begin{equation}
{\bf S}\left( f\right) =-{\Bbb D}\nabla f+{\bf F}f
\end{equation}
${\Bbb D}$ and ${\bf F}$ being respectively the diffusion tensor and
convection vector in phase space. They can be cexpressed generally as 
\begin{equation}
{\Bbb D}=\left( 
\begin{array}{llll}
D_{pp} & D_{p\xi } & D_{p\psi } & D_{p\theta } \\ 
D_{\xi p} & D_{\xi \xi } & D_{\xi \psi } & D_{\xi \theta } \\ 
D_{\psi p} & D_{\psi \xi } & D_{\psi \psi } & D_{\psi \theta } \\ 
D_{\theta p} & D_{\theta \xi } & D_{\theta \psi } & D_{\theta \theta }
\end{array}
\right) 
\end{equation}
and 
\begin{equation}
{\bf F}=\left( 
\begin{array}{l}
F_{p} \\ 
F_{\xi } \\ 
F_{\psi } \\ 
F_{\theta }
\end{array}
\right) 
\end{equation}
where each element is function of $\left( p,\xi ,\psi ,\theta \right) $. We
make the assumption (to be verified for the kincetic equation under
consideration) that all cross-coupling terms are higher order terms and
therefore can be neglected; hence $D_{p\psi }=D_{\psi p}=0,$ $D_{\xi \psi
}=D_{\psi \xi }=0,$ $D_{p\theta }=D_{\theta p}=0$ et $D_{\xi \theta
}=D_{\theta \xi }=0.$ In these conditions, dynamics in momentum and
configuration spaces are decoupled, 
\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial f}{\partial t}+\nabla _{{\bf p}}\cdot {\bf S}_{{\bf p}%
}+\nabla _{{\bf r}}\cdot {\bf S}_{{\bf r}}=0  \label{consform}
\end{equation}
where the fluxes in momentum and configuration space are respectively

\begin{eqnarray}
{\bf S}_{{\bf p}} &=&-{\Bbb D}_{{\bf p}}\nabla _{{\bf p}}f+{\bf F}_{{\bf p}}f
\label{momflux} \\
{\bf S}_{{\bf r}} &=&-{\Bbb D}_{{\bf r}}\nabla _{{\bf r}}f+{\bf F}_{{\bf r}}f
\label{conflux}
\end{eqnarray}

With this ordering assumption, the diffusion tensor becomes 
\begin{equation}
{\Bbb D}=\left( 
\begin{array}{ll}
{\Bbb D}_{{\bf p}} & 0 \\ 
0 & {\Bbb D}_{{\bf r}}
\end{array}
\right) =\left( 
\begin{array}{llll}
D_{pp} & D_{p\xi } & 0 & 0 \\ 
D_{\xi p} & D_{\xi \xi } & 0 & 0 \\ 
0 & 0 & D_{\psi \psi } & D_{\psi \theta } \\ 
0 & 0 & D_{\theta \psi } & D_{\theta \theta }
\end{array}
\right) 
\end{equation}
while the convective vector becomes
\begin{equation}
{\bf F}=\left( 
\begin{array}{l}
{\bf F}_{{\bf p}} \\ 
{\bf F}_{{\bf r}}
\end{array}
\right) =\left( 
\begin{array}{l}
F_{p} \\ 
F_{\xi } \\ 
F_{\psi } \\ 
F_{\theta }
\end{array}
\right) 
\end{equation}
